# Public strawberry benchmark notice

`ALL_benchmark_W60.xlsx` and the derived `benchmark.json.gz` are from the
`NifferLi/Cold-Chain-Transportation-Strawberry` dataset release. Its Hugging
Face dataset card declares the repository license as Apache-2.0. The project
includes the upstream dataset card in `UPSTREAM_README.md` and the Apache-2.0
license text in `LICENSE-2.0.txt`.

The benchmark contains temperature records from six strawberry shipments and
future labels for entering the release authors' rule-defined severe temperature
state. These labels are not laboratory spoilage, sensory-quality, or pathogen
measurements. The source sensor study is Abdella, Brecht, and Uysal (2021),
“A Time-Temperature Dataset for the Strawberry Cold Chain Across Multiple
Shipments and Locations,” arXiv:2103.12895.

This data is from United States shipments. It does not establish performance on
Qatar/GCC routes. See `docs/DATA_CARD.md` for scope and limitations.
