# Fresh strawberries: configured demo profile

## Implemented configuration

The local prototype configures a 2°C reference temperature, 58,000 J/mol activation energy, and 168-hour nominal life. All three are illustrative DEMO_INPUT values. The implemented endpoint is an equivalent-age proxy, not an observed microbial or sensory quality label.

## Research context

The supplied report describes six US strawberry shipments with temperature/location traces and 54 monitored positions, but no direct spoilage endpoint labels. Those data are not present in this workspace and are not loaded by the app.

## Interpretation limit

The displayed strawberry RSL is a configured model estimate. The profile does not claim validated strawberry shelf life, a storage recommendation, a Qatar route, or spoilage-prediction accuracy.
